2026-07-30 — Bayesian BM25 stub corrected (0 new, 3 updated)

While grounding Out-of-Vocabulary, found BM25’s stub description of Bayesian BM25 contradicting the dedicated note. Checked both Doug Turnbull sources (Bayesian BM25 is Cool, Can BM25 be a Probability): BB25 is logistic calibration of the BM25 score into a probability for fusion. The stub is rewritten to match; Bayesian BM25 gained the missing 2026 formulation and the alternatives Turnbull weighs against it.

Decisions

  • The stub’s “useful for out-of-vocabulary and low-frequency terms” claim appears in neither source and was removed; Out-of-Vocabulary had inherited it and was cleaned too. The claim was isolated to that stub.

2026-07-30 — Tokenization and Out-of-Vocabulary (2 new, 14 updated)

Created the two vocabulary-boundary concepts. Tokenization was referenced in ~40 notes and defined in none; it separates analyzer tokenization (open vocabulary, configurable per field, fails by recall loss) from model tokenization (vocabulary fixed at pre-training, fails by OOV). Out-of-Vocabulary consolidates an argument previously re-derived across eight notes and anchors the SPLADE/miniCOIL contrast.

Decisions

  • The “collapse to a single UNK” simplification in Three mistakes when introducing embeddings and vector search left as faithful to its source; Out-of-Vocabulary states the accurate subword-fragmentation mechanism.

2026-07-30 — Brute-force vector search and silent fusion failure (10 new, 27 updated)

  • Hybrid Search’s Elasticsearch section showed the unnormalized bool/should construct with no caveat — caveat added.

2026-07-27 — PCA vs t-SNE for retrieval (1 new, 4 updated)

Created PCA vs t-SNE for Retrieval, giving the mechanism behind a claim the vault only asserted: t-SNE is disqualified twice over — non-parametric (no transform to apply to a query) and a KL objective that does not preserve distances; UMAP proves the disqualifiers independent.
