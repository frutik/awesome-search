# LongFact

A prompt set for benchmarking the factuality of long, multi-paragraph LLM answers, introduced with SAFE in [[Long-form Factuality in Large Language Models]] by Google DeepMind ([[Google]]). Repo: [github.com/google-deepmind/long-form-factuality](https://github.com/google-deepmind/long-form-factuality/tree/main/longfact).

---

## Contents

- **2,280 fact-seeking prompts** across **38 manually selected topics**, grouped into four supercategories: STEM, social sciences, humanities and other.
- Two tasks, **LongFact-Concepts** and **LongFact-Objects**, depending on whether a prompt asks about a concept or an object. Each task has 1,140 prompts, 30 per topic.
- The prompts were generated by GPT-4 and ask for long answers containing many specific facts.

## Why It Exists

Earlier factuality benchmarks such as TruthfulQA, HaluEval, FreshQA, HalluQA and FELM mostly test one short fact per question. FActScore needs long-form answers but covers a single domain, people biographies. LongFact covers long-form answers across many topics.

## How It Is Scored

LongFact has no gold answers. Each response is scored with SAFE, which checks every atomic claim against Google Search, and the results are combined with F1@K. See [[Atomic Claim Verification]]. In the paper's benchmark of 13 models on 250 LongFact-Objects prompts, GPT-4-Turbo, Gemini-Ultra and PaLM-2-L-IT-RLHF scored highest.
