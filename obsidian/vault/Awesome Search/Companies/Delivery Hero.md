# Delivery Hero

Berlin-headquartered online food delivery group operating a portfolio of local brands, including **foodpanda** (Asia), **foodora** (Europe) and **Yemeksepeti** (Turkey). Its engineering and data organisation publishes on the Delivery Hero Tech blog.

The search problem it presents is defined by its portfolio structure: many local brands, many markets, many languages and scripts, and a catalog of restaurants and grocery items whose vocabulary is local rather than global. Search terms arrive as brand nicknames, transliterations and abbreviations with no single canonical form.
