# Allegro

Allegro is an e-commerce platform operating a product catalog spanning 13 departments across four languages — Polish, Czech, Slovak, and Hungarian. Its engineering blog (Allegro Tech) has documented the team's Relevance Assessment Tool (RAT), an LLM-as-a-judge framework for evaluating search relevance at catalog scale.
