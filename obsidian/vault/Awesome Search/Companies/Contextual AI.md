# Contextual AI

AI company whose researchers published [[BlitzRank]], a token-efficient LLM reranking algorithm, and released it as open source.

- GitHub: https://github.com/ContextualAI

## Notable Contributions

**[[BlitzRank]] (2026)** — reranking by tournament graphs. Each listwise LLM call is read as a full set of pairwise preferences, and transitivity fills in the rest. Averaged over 14 TREC DL/BEIR sets and two LLMs, it matched a pairwise LLM reranker's quality at roughly one-eighth of the tokens. Code: https://github.com/ContextualAI/BlitzRank (MIT).
