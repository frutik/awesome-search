# Google

Google's research arms — **Google Research** and **Google DeepMind** — published [[MUVERA]] (Multi-Vector Retrieval via Fixed Dimensional Encodings), which reduces multi-vector [[Late Interaction]] search to single-vector [[Maximum Inner Product Search|MIPS]]. The method was announced on the Google Research blog and has since been implemented in [[Weaviate Vector DB]], [[Qdrant Vector DB]] (via [[FastEmbed]]) and an [[OpenSearch]] sample.
