# InferSense Labs

Solo search and retrieval consultancy founded in 2026 by Monadhika Sharma. Works on [[Search Consultancy|search relevance consulting]], retrieval for [[RAG]] systems and AI assistants, and [[Search Evaluation|search evaluation]], and sells a fixed-scope search relevance audit as its entry engagement.

## Services

- **Search relevance audit** — fixed-scope diagnosis of a search or RAG retrieval system: an evaluation set built from real queries, a ranked list of failure types, and the cheapest fix for each
- **Search relevance and ranking** — [[Query Understanding|query understanding]], [[Hybrid Search|hybrid retrieval]] and [[Learning to Rank|learning to rank]]
- **RAG and retrieval** — chunking, [[Embeddings|embeddings]], hybrid search and reranking for RAG pipelines and agents
- **LLM fine-tuning** — domain-adaptive pre-training, SFT and DPO
- **Search evaluation** — offline metrics, LLM-as-judge pipelines checked against human labels, and A/B tests

## Background

Monadhika Sharma is a named inventor on Walmart patents in search query understanding, including synonym generation for search queries (US10339165B2, granted 2019) and normalization candidates for search queries (US10339134B2, granted 2019). According to the firm's site, Sharma later worked on search quality at Pinterest and on search and recommendations at Roblox, led the ML team at Vantage Discovery through its acquisition by [[Shopify]], and then trained commerce foundation models at Shopify.

## Writing

- [Why embeddings don't work for your search: 7 failure modes and how to fix them](https://infersense-labs.ai/blog/why-embeddings-dont-work/) — failure modes of embedding retrieval (exact identifiers, negation, numbers, multi-constraint queries, query–document asymmetry, domain vocabulary, chunking), with results from NevIR, BEIR and LIMIT, and fixes ordered by effort: evaluation, [[Hybrid Search|hybrid search]] with [[BM25]], query understanding, reranking, then embedding fine-tuning
