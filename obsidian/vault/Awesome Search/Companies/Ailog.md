# Ailog

Vendor of a hosted [[RAG]] product, which also publishes a series of RAG engineering guides on its blog (retrieval, metadata filtering, ensemble retrieval, hybrid fusion, query routing). Its product advertises automatic, feedback-adapted [[Query Routing]] across a customer's data sources, including multi-route when several sources are relevant.
