## 2026-11-09 — A glossary for LLM-as-a-judge terms (1 new, 4 updated)

Agreement, self-consistency and calibration are three different claims about an LLM judge. [[LLM as Judge Vocabulary]] defines each term: the four [[Levels of Judge Agreement|levels of agreement]], inter-judge and ordinal-versus-binary agreement, qrels, learned adjudication, distilled students, two senses of calibration, and juries. Two 2026 arXiv preprints add the evidence. On conversational-RAG context sufficiency, three-label κ of 0.38–0.49 rose to 0.59–0.79 once collapsed to answer-or-abstain, and reading "no gold document" as insufficient evidence lost most of its precision as retrievers improved. CADRE's trained verifier over three specialist judges beat an oracle picking any correct expert label on 18,710 held-out e-commerce pairs, yet letting the judges see each other cost up to 1.76 accuracy points. Both are unreviewed preprints.
