## 2026-08-31 — Etsy Adds LLM-Scored Semantic Relevance to Search Ranking (5 new, 5 updated)

[[Etsy]]'s search models historically ranked on engagement signals (clicks, add-to-carts, purchases) that skew toward already-popular listings regardless of query fit. [[How Etsy Uses LLMs to Improve Search Relevance]] adds [[Semantic Relevance]] as a complementary signal: an [[LLM as Judge|LLM annotator]] anchored to human golden labels scores query-listing pairs, then a three-tier [[Knowledge Distillation|distillation]] cascade — an o3 annotator, a Qwen 3 VL 4B teacher, a BERT two-tower student — compresses that judgment into a model cheap enough for real-time filtering, feature enrichment, loss weighting, and boosting. Fully-relevant listing share rose from 58% to 62% between August and October 2025. Sourced from Etsy's Code as Craft blog, by [[Yuqing Zhang]], [[Congzhe Su]], and [[Susan Liu]].

## 2026-08-31 — Choosing Between RRF and DBSF Fusion in Qdrant (4 new, 7 updated)

[[Reciprocal Rank Fusion|RRF]] discards score magnitude entirely — a document leading by a wide margin scores the same as one leading narrowly, as long as both land at rank 1. [[Qdrant Vector DB|Qdrant]]'s [[Distribution-Based Score Fusion]] keeps the raw scores instead, rescaling each retriever's returned set by its own mean and 3-sigma spread before summing. [[How to Tune Hybrid Search in Qdrant]] measures both across five datasets: DBSF beat default RRF by up to 0.0383 nDCG@10 on [[WANDS Dataset|WANDS]], while RRF's own k parameter shifted results just as much — k=2 and k=61 picked different top results for 42% of WANDS queries. Sourced from [[Dylan Couzon]]'s Qdrant tuning guide and Qdrant's hybrid-queries reference documentation.
