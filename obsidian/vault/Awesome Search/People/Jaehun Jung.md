# Jaehun Jung

University of Washington researcher and first author of *Trust or Escalate*. The paper gives LLM judges a provable guarantee of agreement with humans: each judge abstains when it is not confident, and cases escalate through a cascade of increasingly capable judges.

## Known For
- [[Trust or Escalate - LLM Judges with Provable Guarantees for Human Agreement]] (arXiv:2407.18370, ICLR 2025), with Faeze Brahman and [[Yejin Choi]]. It introduced Cascaded Selective Evaluation and Simulated Annotators.
