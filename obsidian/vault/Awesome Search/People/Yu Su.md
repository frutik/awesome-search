# Yu Su

## Background

Senior author on [[HippoRAG]] (NeurIPS 2024), which models retrieval on the hippocampal indexing theory of human long-term memory, combining an LLM, a knowledge graph and Personalized PageRank.

## Articles in This Vault

- [[HippoRAG - Neurobiologically Inspired Long-Term Memory for Large Language Models]] — with Bernal Jiménez Gutiérrez, Yiheng Shu, Yu Gu and Michihiro Yasunaga

## Key Contributions

- [[HippoRAG]] — single-step multi-hop retrieval reaching parity with iterative retrieval at 10–30× lower cost and 6–13× higher speed
