# Jaime Arguello

Information retrieval researcher known for work on aggregated search. As lead author of the SIGIR 2009 paper *Sources of Evidence for Vertical Selection*, written at Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute with Yahoo! Labs collaborators, he defined [[Vertical Selection]] around three evidence sources — the query string, per-vertical query logs, and vertical-representative corpora — and showed query logs to be the strongest single signal.
