# Themis Mavridis

ranking-at-scale paper and of the widely-cited *150 Successful Machine Learning
Models: 6 Lessons Learned at Booking.com* (Bernardi, Mavridis & Estevez, KDD
2019), which makes him a recurring name in Booking.com's published ML work.
