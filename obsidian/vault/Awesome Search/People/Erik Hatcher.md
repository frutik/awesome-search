# Erik Hatcher

Search engineer at [[MongoDB]]. Author of the MongoDB Hybrid Search series, which runs from a survey of the findability landscape through the classic fusion mechanisms to [[Semantic Boosting]], the two-phase hybrid retrieval technique he names and describes.

His recurring theme across the series is the discipline rather than the algorithm: **measure, tune, repeat**, and a running check of whether the added complexity is worth it — "is the juice worth the squeeze?" He defines [[Hybrid Search]] from experience rather than the literature, as *combining two or more search techniques to produce results better than any single technique alone*, and insists the word "better" is what obliges you to measure.

Known for practical, implementation-focused search content — working with Lucene, Solr, and Atlas Search.
