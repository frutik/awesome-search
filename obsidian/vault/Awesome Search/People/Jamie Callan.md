# Jamie Callan

Information retrieval researcher at Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute. Co-author of the SIGIR 2009 paper on [[Vertical Selection]], which compared a supervised classifier against single-evidence baselines drawn from distributed IR and [[Federated Search]] resource selection — ReDDE, Soft.ReDDE and Clarity — and query-log likelihood.
