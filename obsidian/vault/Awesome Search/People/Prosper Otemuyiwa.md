## Contribution to This Vault

- [[How to Use Jev - A Practical Guide]] (2026-09-17) — a working guide to [[TypeSafe]]'s
  [[System One Model|System One]] API: the three question primitives, five usage patterns, the
  documented failure modes, and a sceptical reading of TypeSafe's own benchmark.

Two parts earn their place here. The piece collects TypeSafe's "jaggedness" page — what the
model is bad at — into the clearest summary of those limits in this vault. And its
retrieve-then-judge pattern states a limit on calibrated scoring that the vendor material does
not: a model that knows only the state you hand it will return a well-calibrated judgment about
bad evidence if that is what it was given, so whatever assembles the state sets the ceiling on
everything downstream.

The scepticism is real but the placement should be noted — the article runs on the account of a
retrieval vendor whose product supplies the retrieval half of that pattern.
