# Pat Verga

Researcher at [[Cohere]] and first author of the PoLL paper, which argues for judging LLM output with a panel of smaller models from different families instead of one large judge.

## Known For
- [[Replacing Judges with Juries]] (arXiv:2404.18796, 2024): the Panel of LLM evaluators (PoLL). It agreed with human labels better than GPT-4, showed less self-preference bias, and cost seven to eight times less.
