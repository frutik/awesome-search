# Thierry Damiba

Developer relations engineer at [[Qdrant]]. Author of the five-part [[Fine-Tuning Sparse Embeddings for E-Commerce Search]] series (March 2026), which fine-tunes [[SPLADE]] on the [[Amazon ESCI Dataset]], benchmarks it against [[BM25]], tests cross-catalog transfer, and packages the pipeline as [[qdrant-sparse-finetune]].

The series is notable for reporting its negative results as prominently as its positive ones — the ESCI-tuned model loses to off-the-shelf SPLADE on Home Depot data and collapses on [[MS MARCO]].
