# Dima Kan

## Background

Dima Kan is Product Director of Search at Aiven, a managed database platform. He is the founder and host of [[Vector Podcast]], launched in October 2021 to interview the creators of the emerging vector-search wave.

He spotted that wave early: days before the podcast started he published ["Not All Vector Databases Are Made Equal"](https://dmitry-kan.medium.com/milvus-pinecone-vespa-weaviate-vald-gsi-what-unites-these-buzz-words-and-what-makes-each-9c65a3bd0696) on Medium — a side-by-side of Milvus, [[Pinecone]], [[Vespa]], [[Weaviate]], Vald, GSI and [[Qdrant]] written with people from each of those teams, which he reports has been read over 120K times.

He also authored the article introducing Wormhole Vectors and their implementation in OpenSearch, applying [[Trey Grainger]]'s research to production architecture.
