# Negar Arabzadeh

Information retrieval researcher working on evaluation methodology, and in particular on how [[LLM as Judge|LLM-based relevance judgments]] behave when they are used in place of human assessors.

## Known For
- [[Benchmarking LLM-based Relevance Judgment Methods]] (arXiv:2504.12558, 2025) with [[Charles L. A. Clarke]] — a side-by-side benchmark of binary, graded, pairwise-preference and nugget-based LLM judgments, showing that agreement with human labels and agreement with human system rankings are maximized by different methods
