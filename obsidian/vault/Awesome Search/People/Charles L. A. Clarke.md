# Charles L. A. Clarke

Information retrieval researcher working on evaluation methodology, including the reliability of [[LLM as Judge|LLM-generated relevance judgments]] as a substitute for human assessment.

## Known For
- [[Benchmarking LLM-based Relevance Judgment Methods]] (arXiv:2504.12558, 2025) with [[Negar Arabzadeh]] — establishes that no single LLM judgment paradigm is best on both label agreement and system-ranking agreement

- Co-organizer of the [[LLMJudge]] challenge at the LLM4Eval workshop, SIGIR 2024
