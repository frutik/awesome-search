# Jean Silva

Search engineer at [[Wallapop]]. He started out as a software engineer and has worked on search in travel and e-commerce before moving to classified ads. At [[Haystack EU]] 2023 he co-presented with [[Rene Kriegler]] on how Wallapop reduces [[Zero Results]].

---

## Known For

- Wallapop's first production approach to [[Query Relaxation]]: drop the query term with the lowest document frequency. He built the offline dataset from search logs and tokens' document frequencies in [[Solr]], and reached about 80% offline accuracy before an A/B test.
