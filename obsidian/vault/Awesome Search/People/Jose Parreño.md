# Jose Parreño

Sr. Data Science Manager at [[Skyscanner]], writing on data science leadership,
storytelling, and applied machine learning. Publishes paper reviews on Medium at
[@joparga3](https://medium.com/@joparga3).

## Position

Reviewing Booking.com's ranking-at-scale paper, Parreño argues its value is not
confined to organisations operating at that scale — he calls it "a gold mine of
how to start with any ranking project." His framing emphasis is that **defining
the target variable is the foundational difficulty** in ranking and
recommendation, prior to any modelling choice. See [[Ranking Signal Selection]].
