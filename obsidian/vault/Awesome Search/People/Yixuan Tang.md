# Yixuan Tang

Information retrieval researcher at the **National University of Singapore**, working on diversity-aware retrieval — surfacing complementary perspectives on an event rather than the most textually similar reporting of it.

## Known For

- [[Uncovering the Bigger Picture - Comprehensive Event Understanding via Diverse News Retrieval]] (EMNLP 2025), first author — the NEWSCOPE framework, which models retrieval diversity at the sentence level rather than over whole-document embeddings, and the Positive Cluster Coverage and Information Density Ratio metrics
