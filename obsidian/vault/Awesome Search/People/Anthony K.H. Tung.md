# Anthony K.H. Tung

Researcher at the **National University of Singapore**, working on fine-grained semantic modeling for retrieval diversity.

## Known For

- [[Uncovering the Bigger Picture - Comprehensive Event Understanding via Diverse News Retrieval]] (EMNLP 2025), final author — NEWSCOPE, a two-stage framework that clusters sentences to make aspect coverage, rather than embedding distance, the target of diversification
- [[Diversity-Aware k-Maximum Inner Product Search Revisited]] (arXiv 2024; VLDB Journal 2026), co-author — DkMIPS, diversity-aware [[Maximum Inner Product Search]] with Ball-Cone Tree pruning, and its news demo DiversiNews (PVLDB 2024)
