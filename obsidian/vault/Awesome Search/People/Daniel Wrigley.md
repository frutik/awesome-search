# Daniel Wrigley

Search relevance practitioner and writer on search quality measurement. Author of the Elastic Search Labs guide on building collaborative relevance [[Judgment Lists]] with [[Quepid]].

Co-authored the [[OpenSearch]] project's announcement of the [[OpenSearch Relevance Agent]] with [[Bobby Mohammed]] — relevance diagnosis and query-DSL tuning driven conversationally, with metric computation deliberately held outside the LLM.

## Known For
