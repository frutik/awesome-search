# Qiang Huang

Researcher at the **National University of Singapore** School of Computing, working on inner-product and point-to-hyperplane similarity search. Code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/HuangQiang).

## Known For

- [[Diversity-Aware k-Maximum Inner Product Search Revisited]] (arXiv 2024; extended in The VLDB Journal, 2026), first author. It reformulates diversity-aware [[Maximum Inner Product Search]] with average and maximum pairwise-similarity objectives, the Greedy and DualGreedy algorithms, and Ball-Cone Tree pruning.
- The **Ball-Cone Tree (BC-Tree)**, a lightweight Ball-Tree variant for point-to-hyperplane nearest-neighbour search (with [[Anthony K.H. Tung]], ICDE 2023), which DkMIPS reuses as its index.
- DiversiNews (PVLDB 2024), co-author and corresponding author — the news-retrieval demo of DkMIPS.
