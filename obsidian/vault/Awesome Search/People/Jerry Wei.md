# Jerry Wei

Google DeepMind ([[Google]]) researcher and first author of the long-form factuality paper. It introduced the [[LongFact]] prompt set, SAFE (an LLM agent that checks each claim with Google Search) and the F1@K metric.

## Known For
- [[Long-form Factuality in Large Language Models]] (arXiv:2403.18802, NeurIPS 2024). SAFE agreed with crowd annotators on 72% of facts, was right in 76% of 100 sampled disagreements, and cost more than 20× less.
