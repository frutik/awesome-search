# Jonathan Larson

## Background

Partner Data Architect at Microsoft Research, co-author of the post that introduced [[GraphRAG]] — the approach of building an LLM-generated knowledge graph over a private corpus and retrieving over its structure rather than over independent passage embeddings.

## Articles in This Vault

- [[GraphRAG - Unlocking LLM discovery on narrative private data]] — with [[Steven Truitt]]

## Key Contributions

- Named and popularised [[GraphRAG]]
- Articulated the two baseline-RAG failure modes it addresses: connecting disparate information, and holistic semantic understanding across a collection
